📖 How To Play — Horizon Forge
🎯 Objective

Win most battles in a duel to advance. In Best of 3, win 2 battles. In Best of 5, win 3. The opponent keeps the same army for the whole duel — analyze it before building yours.

💰 Gold
🏁
Each duel starts with 7💰.
⚔️
Between battles you receive gold automatically — a bonus if you won the previous battle.
🔄
New Recruitment costs 2💰.
⛺ Recruitment & Bench
🆕
Buying a character you don't own yet costs 3💰. They go straight to the Barracks.
🔗
Buying a character you already own costs 2💰 and stacks on the same card — each hero uses only 1 slot in the Barracks.
🖱️
Drag a card from Barracks to a field slot, or click it then click the slot.
✨ Card Merging
⭐
Stack 3 copies of the same character to auto-merge — they level up (★ → ★★ → ★★★...) and become much more powerful.
⚠️
Selling a card gives gold based on its level. Unmerged copies are lost.
